description: Teach one concept from the course material, then check it
argument-hint: <topic>

Explain to the student: $ARGUMENTS

1. Find it in courses/*/material/ and cite the file.
2. Teach in at most 6 short bullets: the rule, one concrete example from the material, one common trap.
3. If it can be shown in code or a quick calculation, show a tiny snippet or worked example.
4. Check understanding with ONE question. Wait. Correct only what they got wrong.
5. Log it (type "study") and add the topic to data/weak-spots.json if they struggled.
